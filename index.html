Privacy Policy

This application is used to manage an eBay seller account through the eBay API.

The application may access data required to manage listings, inventory, orders, fulfilment, shipping information, and related seller operations.

Data is used only for the operation of the application and is not sold to third parties.

Authentication is handled through eBay OAuth. The application does not receive or store the user's eBay password.

Access tokens and related credentials are used only to communicate securely with eBay APIs.
